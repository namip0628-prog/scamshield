TRANSPARENCY

What ScamShield can—and can’t—tell you.

ScamShield is an independent screening aid. It is not a bank, government service, or law-enforcement authority.

HOW CHECKS WORK

Warning signs, not certainty

Checks use explicit message patterns and indicators from reports reviewed by a ScamShield moderator. The 0–100 score is a heuristic signal, not a probability, identity check, or guarantee.

A report marked verified means a moderator accepted it for the community indicator list. It does not establish legal proof or confirm the identity of a sender.

PRIVACY

What is stored

Text entered into the checker is analyzed for the response and is not saved as a check or attached to your account. Reports you submit are stored so moderators can review them; reports submitted while signed in are associated with your account.

Do not submit OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords, full card details, or other sensitive financial information. Use only contact details found independently through your bank or provider’s official channels.

COMMUNITY DATA

What trends represent

Dashboard counts include only reports accepted by moderators. They represent submissions received by ScamShield, not the prevalence of scams in India. Multiple reports may describe the same campaign.

IF YOU LOST MONEY

Act through official channels

Contact your bank immediately using its official app or published number. In India, call cyber fraud helpline 1930 and report at cybercrime.gov.in.
